## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive-grade, hyper-focused aesthetic tailored for high-stakes interview preparation. It merges the gravitas of a private corporate coaching suite with the subtle kinetic feedback of an intelligent, perceptual AI copilot.

The emotional goal is composure under pressure: tranquil, authoritative, and optically non-distracting. To support extended rehearsal sessions without visual fatigue, the system adopts a Dark Minimalist core accented by frosted Glassmorphic surfaces and precise luminescent telemetry indicators.

### Key Visual Tenets
- **Deep Immersion:** Inky void-level canvases allow simulated video feeds, metrics, and transcription streams to command attention without chrome competition.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Neon accents (electric blue, cyan, indigo) are never decorative; they strictly communicate AI cognition, audio capture levels, speaking pace, and primary calls to action.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Borders are razor-sharp hairline dividers that catch faint ambient light rather than heavy opaque strokes.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around low-reflectance, high-contrast dark values, ensuring crisp legibility under low ambient room lighting.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3B82F6` - Electric Blue):** Represents platform confidence, active execution, and primary interactive affordances.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Cyan Stream):** Dictates live telemetry, speech detection waveforms, microphone activity, and positive analytical feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1` - Deep Indigo):** Signifies synthetic processing, automated scoring engines, and latent AI background computation.
- **Neutral Base (`#0B0F17`):** The foundational obsidian canvas.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Viewport root and non-interactive backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111827`):** Structural panels, sidebars, dock rails, and standard inactive cards.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Hover states, floating control modals, transcript pill containers, and video canvas bezels.
- **Border / Hairline Subtle (`#1E293B`):** Structural dividing rules.
- **Border / Highlight Active (`#334155`):** Focused card contours, active tab outlines, and key input boundaries.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Display headings and active metrics.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Analytical descriptors, timestamps, and muted body copy.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scanning and high legibility during active speech and time-constrained simulations.

- **Systemic Unity:** Powered across all roles by **Inter**, chosen for its tall x-height, neutral tone, and exceptional screen-raster clarity at small data densities.
- **Numerical Telemetry:** All live metrics (countdown clocks, speaking cadence charts, filler-word counters) must use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to avoid layout jitter during live calculations.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-labels and AI status flags leverage `label-caps` in uppercase with deliberate tracking to distinguish diagnostic output from conversational transcripts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is divided into two operational environments:

### 1. The HUD / Session Cockpit (No-scroll, Fixed-aspect)
A responsive viewport grid dedicated to simulated interviews. Center-stage is reserved for camera and avatar feeds, bounded by an asymmetrical vertical dock (sidebar) for conversational telemetry and dynamic questioning scripts. Gutters are locked to `space-md` (16px) to maximize real-estate efficiency.

### 2. The Analytics & Prep Dashboard (Fluid 12-Column Grid)
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and 32px (`margin`) outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutters and 24px margins. Transcripts collapse beneath the primary viewport.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with single-column analytical stacks. Bottom-anchored controls handle live inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical translucency, layered dark values, and ambient edge highlights rather than traditional high-cast drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Inky Canvas (`#0B0F17`). Pure solid, non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Workspaces):** Flat `#111827` overlaid with a 1px perimeter outline of `#1E293B`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Dynamic Feed Cards):** Dark slate `#1E293B` or frosted glass (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Bound by a 1px top/lateral stroke of `#334155`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Float Action Bars):** Elevated floating surfaces (`#1E293B`) paired with an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`.

### Active AI Glows
When speech parsing is initiated, active cards or audio rings project an ambient cyan/blue feather: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between institutional sharpness and modern consumer fluidity using roundedness tier `2`.

- **Standard Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Tiles):** Base radius of `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Cards, Camera Bezels, and Core Modules:** Scaled up to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to create soft frames around video feeds and performance scorecards.
- **Status Badges, Recording Chips, and Mic Docks:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to visually set real-time runtime indicators apart from static grid panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Start Session, Complete Prep):** Solid electric blue (`#3B82F6`) text on pure white or high-contrast foreground, transitioning to hover state `#2563EB` with an ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`). Radius: 12px.
- **Secondary (Skip, Practice Drill):** Deep slate fill (`#1E293B`) with a hairline border (`#334155`) and primary text (`#F8FAFC`). Hover brings border luminosity to `#64748B`.
- **Destructive / Stop Recording:** Deep muted rose surface with sharp white text; crimson warning glow on hover.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Compact pill-shaped modules (`height: 28px`, `px: 12px`, `label-caps`).
- **Live State:** Frosted dark navy backing with an animated pulsing cyan point (`#06B6D4`) denoting speech-to-text intake.
- **Metric State:** Transparent backing, border `#334155`, text `#94A3B8`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Solid `#111827` surface framed by `#1E293B` borders. Radius: 10px.
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- **Focus Ring:** Precise hairline transition to `#3B82F6` accompanied by an electric halo: `0 0 0 1px #3B82F6, 0 0 12px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Structural cards employ `#111827` backgrounds, 16px border radiuses, and low-contrast borders (`#1E293B`).
- Cards containing actionable AI critiques feature a subtle top border gradient fading from `#3B82F6` on the left to transparent on the right.

### Audio / Video Session Cockpit (Platform-Specific)
- **Video Feeds:** Anchored inside rounded 16px containers with deep `#0B0F17` letterboxing and an inner inset stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **AI Waveform Monitor:** Oscilloscope visualizer utilizing gradient stroke sequences (`#3B82F6` through `#06B6D4`) on frosted floating panels.
- **Floating Controls:** Centered bottom-dock pill (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)` + blur) holding circular mic, camera, and session toggles.

### Iconography
- Line style only (Lucide-inspired), calibrated to a consistent 1.75px stroke weight, framed in 20x20px or 24x24px bounding boxes, matching text color hierarchies.
